# Code of Conduct

PanoPose is a small technical project. Contributions and discussions should stay professional, specific, and respectful.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct without being hostile.
- Assume good intent, but correct mistakes clearly.
- Keep issue and pull request discussion focused on the software.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, architecture, and release timing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic comments after being asked to stop.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments; close issues or pull requests; block users; or take other reasonable action to keep the project usable and safe.

To report a conduct issue, contact the maintainer privately.
